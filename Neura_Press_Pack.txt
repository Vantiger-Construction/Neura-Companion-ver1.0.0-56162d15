Neura Companion – Press Kit Overview

Tagline: Your AI. Your World. Your Future.

Summary: Neura Companion is a highly adaptive artificial intelligence app that learns, evolves, and grows with its user. Combining voice, mood tracking, visual animation, and immersive dream simulation, it provides emotional support, guidance, and connection in a beautifully futuristic way.

Highlights:
- Voice + Viseme Sync Engine
- Dream Visualizer + Reflective Journal
- Hologram Mode + VR Room Support
- Global MoodMap + Personality AI Core
- Android, Windows, Web support

Assets included: Logos, screenshots, launch trailer, sample APK/EXE/web demo, feature breakdown PDF.
