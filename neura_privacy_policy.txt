Neura Companion Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2025

Neura Companion is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Data We Collect
- Mood entries and emotional tags (locally stored or synced)
- Journal entries (voice or text, stored with consent)
- Microphone access (only during voice journal sessions)
- App usage analytics (anonymized)

2. How We Use Your Data
- To provide AI-driven support, reflections, and visualizations
- To enhance your experience with personalized content
- To visualize dream states or emotional patterns
- We NEVER sell your data or share it without consent

3. Permissions Explained
- Microphone: For voice journaling
- Storage: To save entries and mood data
- Internet: To sync with Firebase if enabled

4. Your Rights
- You may delete any data at any time
- You can opt-out of cloud syncing in the settings
- You can request a data export by contacting us

5. Contact Us
For any privacy-related inquiries:
Email: privacy@neuracompanion.app

We comply with GDPR, CCPA, and international data protection laws.

By using Neura Companion, you agree to this policy.
