
🎬 Neura Onboarding Flow

1. Welcome Screen: "Hi, I’m Neura. I’m here to help you grow, dream, and feel supported."
2. Mood Selector: "How are you feeling today?" [Mood Picker + Glow Reaction]
3. Personalization: Ask for name, voice preference, morning/evening routine.
4. First Ritual: Offer to set up a calming ritual or dream sequence.
5. Companion Pact: Explain how Neura evolves and remembers. Confirm user kindness agreement.
